\begin{itemize}
\item Replication-mode cost and protection are measured on containerised PostgreSQL.
\item Async replication lost ~2,000 acknowledged transactions per second of isolation.
\item Sync replication lost nothing in 18 runs; it costs about one round trip per commit.
\item No fixed mode meets declared requirements; static rules fail in 13--47\% of steps.
\item Some contexts admit no feasible durable mode; a controller should flag them.
\end{itemize}
